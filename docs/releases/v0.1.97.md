# v0.1.97 Release Notes

## A new look for the phone app

- One home instead of two tabs. A glass chip on top picks the project (its
  pixel mascot, or every project); under **Tasks** a colored line says how
  many need you and how many run, and with a project picked, what its
  worktrees changed (`2 worktrees · 6 files +156 −28`). Only what needs you
  is an amber card; running and recent tasks are quiet rows.
- The Workspaces tab became a sheet from the project chip: every project
  with what waits and runs there, the picked one open to its worktrees and
  **New task here**. Search for projects, worktrees and tasks lives there.
- **New task** is the composer itself: the project's mascot over a dither in
  its color asks what to do, and agent and model, access, worktree and
  auto-resume are chips. A composer docked at the bottom of the home opens it
  in the project you picked.
- A conversation runs under a floating glass header with a chip for the
  branch and its changes (`feat/x · 3 files +78 −14`). The composer floats
  as a capsule and rises with the keyboard. Code blocks show their language,
  a copy button and light highlighting; your own messages turn neutral.
- **Changes** opens from that chip: uncommitted and branch changes, each file
  with its +/−, its diff a tap away and the whole file full screen with line
  numbers. Commit and push sit in a fixed footer; pull request actions live in
  the PR card.
- Slide a running task to stop it, a recent one to archive it; hold any row
  for open, changes, complete and archive. Lists animate, approvals and sends
  give haptic feedback, and loading shows the robot walking over the dither.
- Light and dark follow the system. On iOS 26 the floating controls use
  Liquid Glass.

## Desktop

- The phone can open a whole file of a task's workspace
  (`GET /api/v1/mobile/workspaces/:id/file`): text up to 512 KB, binaries
  flagged, and paths that resolve outside the workspace refused. The phone
  asks to update the desktop when the route is missing.

## Fixes

- Sheets on the phone keep their header visible above the list, and edge
  fades no longer cover the top of a screen.
