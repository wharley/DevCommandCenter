# v0.3.9 Release Notes

## The room, from the request to the delivery

- "Origem" on a task an agent opened: who asked for it and through whom, on
  the task's head and on its pull request ("Você pediu a Dev", a source, a
  routine, the agents it passed through). An address pasted in the request
  shows without its user and password.
- Work passed along a chain comes back: when the last agent answers, the
  agent the request started with hears the result, once, and closes with you.
- After you merge a task an agent opened, DCC reads from GitHub whether the
  merge commit reached production and tells the agent the work started with:
  it tells you, and when the request came from a connector it proposes the
  reply there as a card you send. "Em produção" is only said with a
  production deployment of that commit; elsewhere it says "mesclado". The
  merge stays yours.
- The lines of one piece of work read as one subject: the request, the
  answer, the pass, its result, the delivery and the closing word are one
  block that opens and closes, with where it stands on its head (em
  andamento, aguardando você, respondido, mesclado, em produção). Each agent
  keeps a single conversation, with all its context.
- "Responde: …" on the room's head: who answers a message that names nobody,
  per room. A mention, a review's words and whoever was just talking still
  come first.
- "Responder" on an agent's answer: the next message goes to that agent and
  opens with the answer's start as a quote.
- "Enviar agora" on a queued message sends it into the turn at work, where
  the agent's provider takes guidance mid-turn.
- A task's report shows once, under the last answer that speaks of it.

## Finding and reading in the room

- Find in the room (⌘F), also inside cards and on an agent's page; the
  session search reaches the rooms.
- The room's text can be selected and copied.
- A click on an agent's face or name goes to its conversation; a hover still
  shows its card.

## Sources and routines

- "Nova fonte" is a form: the connector, its tool, the arguments, how often
  and which room, with "✨" to suggest the arguments and a test before
  saving. A source has "Editar".
- An agent's routines can be read and changed by the agent itself, through
  DCC's tools.

## Speed

- A Claude agent's conversation in a room stays warm between turns for ten
  minutes, so the next message does not start the agent again.
- The row of an agent that was just asked says it is waiting, and the first
  sign of its answer shows sooner.

## Phone

- The room on the phone words the new lines (a chain's result, a delivery)
  as the desktop does. No update of the phone app is needed.
